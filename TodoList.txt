The way my group plays is that after the first kingdom, each player (usually 4-5 players) can remove one card from the current kingdom, except for the winner who removes two cards. Then we add new cards in randomly. So approximately 50% of the kingdom is refreshed every game. Two features that would be very helpful to support this play style would be:

- Duplicate a kingdom
- That way we could start from the same kingdom we had from the last game.

- Edit a kingdom
- That way we could strike cards from the duplicated kingdom and randomize in the new cards.
